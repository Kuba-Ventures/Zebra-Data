HIPAA & SOC 2 in progress · End-to-end encryption · Zero patient data retention by default
The problem

Healthcare data is broken into a thousand pieces.

Most clinical teams stitch together a patient's history from a dozen systems that don't talk to each other. The result is duplicated work, missed signals, and worse outcomes.

01

Fragmented EMR access

Records live behind a patchwork of Epic, Cerner, athena, and dozens of regional systems — each with its own auth, format, and gatekeeper. Teams spend hours reassembling histories that should be one query.

02

Unstructured clinical data

The most important signals — discharge summaries, progress notes, faxed referrals — are buried in PDFs and free text. Without structured extraction, they're invisible to every downstream workflow.

03

Entity resolution across systems

Is "Maria Garcia" in Epic the same person as "M. Garcia-Lopez" in the lab feed? Probabilistic matching at scale is hard — and getting it wrong has clinical consequences.

97%

of hospital data goes unused. It exists — generated every minute by EMRs, labs, devices, and notes — but it never reaches the point of care where it could change a decision.

Why Zebra

Built for the messiest data in the most regulated industry.

Other platforms treat healthcare like just another data integration problem. We built Zebra around what actually breaks: identity, language, context, and trust.

AI-powered entity resolution

Probabilistic matching trained on real-world clinical data — across name variants, address moves, and missing identifiers. Audit-ready confidence scores on every match.

Language & culturally aware navigation

Care navigation that meets patients where they are — in their preferred language, with cultural and social context built into the routing logic, not bolted on after.

API-first architecture

Every capability is an API. FHIR-native endpoints, webhooks, and event streams — so you can drop Zebra into the systems you already run, instead of replacing them.

Workflow-ready, not just data-ready

Purpose-built for care management and scheduling. Outcomes, not just integrations — so your clinical and ops teams feel the lift in week one.

Use cases

From a unified record to the workflows that actually move outcomes.

Care management

Route the right patient to the right specialist — automatically.

Zebra reads the full longitudinal record to triage patients into the right care pathway, manage post-visit follow-ups, and surface gaps in care before they become readmissions.

  • Specialist routing based on history & geography
  • Post-discharge follow-up orchestration
  • Gaps-in-care signals delivered to the EMR
Intelligent scheduling

Agentic outreach that books appointments, not callbacks.

An always-on scheduling layer that proactively reaches patients in their preferred language, finds the right slot, and books it — for new visits, follow-ups, and discharge care alike.

  • Multilingual SMS & voice agents
  • Real-time scheduler integration (Epic, athena)
  • Discharge follow-up booked before the patient leaves
